The Best Free Apps You Should Download Right Now

From productivity to personal finance, language learning to creative tools — these are the free apps that are genuinely worth your time in 2025.

⏱ 6 min read 📅 June 2025 👤 BrainFuel Team

Productivity

Notion — All-in-one workspace

Notion is one of the most flexible productivity apps ever made. You can use it as a to-do list, a personal journal, a project manager, a note-taking tool, a database, or all of the above at the same time. It is free for personal use with no time limit, and works on every device — phone, tablet, and computer. If you currently use five different apps to stay organised, Notion can replace all of them.

Todoist — Simple task management

If Notion feels overwhelming, Todoist is the simpler alternative. It is a clean, no-nonsense task manager that works beautifully on every device. The free plan includes unlimited tasks, labels, and filters. It even understands natural language — type "Buy groceries tomorrow at 3pm" and it will automatically set the date and time.

Google Keep — Quick notes

For capturing quick thoughts, shopping lists, reminders, and ideas, Google Keep is hard to beat. It is completely free, syncs across all your devices instantly, and lets you set location-based reminders — so it reminds you to buy milk when you are actually near the supermarket.

💡
Which should you use?

Use Google Keep for quick captures, Todoist for daily task management, and Notion if you want a full personal knowledge system. Many people use all three for different purposes.

Finance

Money Manager — Personal budgeting

Money Manager is a straightforward, free app for tracking your income and expenses. You log what you spend, categorise it (food, transport, bills, etc.), and the app shows you clear reports of where your money is going. It is particularly popular in Asian markets and has a clean, easy-to-understand interface.

Google Sheets — Custom budgets

For those who want more control, Google Sheets (available free on iOS and Android) is a powerful option. You can build your own budget tracker exactly how you want it — and since it syncs to the cloud, you can access it anywhere. Search for "budget template Google Sheets" and you will find hundreds of free, ready-made templates to get started instantly.

Learning

Duolingo — Language learning

Duolingo is the world's most downloaded education app — and its free version is genuinely excellent. It teaches over 40 languages using short, game-like lessons that take just 5 minutes a day. Whether you want to learn Spanish, Japanese, French, or even Welsh, Duolingo makes it surprisingly enjoyable and habit-forming.

Khan Academy — Everything else

Khan Academy offers free courses in maths, science, computing, history, economics, and more — all completely free, forever. It is used by students and adults worldwide, and the content quality is genuinely excellent. It also has dedicated SAT and GMAT prep for those preparing for university entrance exams.

Security

Bitwarden — Password manager

Bitwarden is the best free password manager available. It stores all your passwords securely, generates strong new ones, and fills them in automatically across all your devices. It is open-source — meaning security experts can inspect the code — and the free plan includes unlimited passwords with sync across unlimited devices. There is simply no reason to use weak or repeated passwords when Bitwarden exists.

ProtonMail — Private email

ProtonMail is an email service that is encrypted end-to-end, meaning not even Proton can read your emails. It is based in Switzerland, subject to strict privacy laws. If you are concerned about the privacy of your regular Gmail or Outlook account, ProtonMail's free plan gives you a private, secure alternative for sensitive communications.

Photos & Memories

Google Photos — Unlimited photo backup

Google Photos is one of the most useful free apps on any phone. It automatically backs up your photos and videos to the cloud — so even if you lose or break your phone, your memories are safe. Google Photos also has excellent AI search (find all photos with "beach" or "my dog" or "2022 birthday") and smart albums that organise your photos automatically.

📌
Note:

Google Photos offers 15GB of free storage (shared with your Google account). For most people this lasts several years. iPhone users also have iCloud Photos, which offers 5GB free — enough to get started.

Reading & Information

Pocket — Save articles to read later

Found an interesting article but don't have time to read it now? Pocket lets you save it with one click and read it later — even without an internet connection. It strips away ads and distractions, giving you a clean reading experience. It is ideal for people who want to read more but never seem to find the time.

Wikipedia — Knowledge, free forever

Simple but essential — the Wikipedia app gives you fast, clean access to the world's largest free encyclopedia. Works offline for saved articles, and is one of the most reliable quick-reference tools available on any device.

Creativity

Canva — Design for everyone

Canva lets anyone create professional-quality designs — social media posts, posters, presentations, flyers, logos, and more — without any design experience. The free plan includes thousands of templates, fonts, and images. It is used by small businesses, students, teachers, and content creators around the world.

CapCut — Video editing

CapCut is the video editing app behind most of the polished videos you see on TikTok and Instagram. It is free, surprisingly powerful, and easy enough for complete beginners. You can trim clips, add music, apply filters, add subtitles automatically, and use AI to enhance your video — all from your phone.
